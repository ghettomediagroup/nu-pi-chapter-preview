Signature Program

Talent Hunt

The Talent Hunt Program is one of Omega Psi Phi's signature initiatives, cultivating and showcasing young performing artists in music, dance, and the dramatic arts. Chapter winners advance to district competition.

Talent Hunt gives young people a stage, an audience, and a path toward scholarship support for their craft.

Civic Engagement

Social Action

Through the Social Action Program, Nu Pi advances voter registration and education, civic advocacy, health awareness, and mentorship, meeting the needs of our community and standing up for its interests.

Community Service

Year-round, the brothers of Nu Pi give their time and resources to the community, from the annual Toy Drive and Golf Outing to partnerships with local schools and organizations across Joliet.
